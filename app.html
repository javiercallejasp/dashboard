What happens next

The same pipeline that runs after every Google Meet.
Transcript is sent to your n8n workflow
Gemini analyzes participants, decisions, tasks and risks
Rows are written to Google Sheets
Each owner gets a Slack message and an email
This dashboard refreshes automatically

🔌 n8n connection

Actions (status changes, reminders, new tasks, transcripts) are sent to your n8n webhooks.
